## Rules for future evaluations

1. Record licence, last meaningful maintenance, language/runtime, security exposure and commercial-use terms.
2. Prefer small pure functions and test fixtures over importing complete applications.
3. Validate any adopted geometry or nesting method against ULTIDA's scene-first golden fixtures.
4. Do not ingest third-party catalogue images or models without provenance and usage rights.
5. Keep images and AI results as reviewable visual evidence; measurements come from accepted geometry and production rules.
